3. Custom Domains, Payments, Refunds & Withdrawal

3.1 Fee, quotes & coverage

Managed custom short domains cost $10 USD per domain per year, payable in Bitcoin. Each domain receives its own unique payment address, so multiple domains may be paid in any order. A quote is valid for 7 days; requesting a new quote for the same domain re-uses the same address. Send the exact quoted amount — the Bitcoin network fee is separate and paid by you. Underpayments must be topped up to the same address; overpayments or payments to expired addresses are handled case by case through the Report page.

Your domain activates after the payment's first blockchain confirmation (unconfirmed payments never activate; partial payments are shown so you can top up). Coverage lasts one year and early renewals stack remaining time on top. If coverage lapses, links on that domain stop resolving until renewed; deleting a domain permanently deletes its links and statistics. Discount codes, where issued, are single-use unless stated otherwise, bound to the first domain redeeming them, may carry an expiry date, have no cash value, cannot be transferred, and removing a code returns the domain to full price.

All refunds are at the administration's sole discretion following its review and are not guaranteed. Nothing here limits your mandatory statutory rights, including the withdrawal right in §3.3. No refunds are due for discount-code activations, as no payment was made, nor for miner/network fees, which we never receive.
